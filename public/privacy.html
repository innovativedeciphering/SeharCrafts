Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This policy explains how Sehar Crafts handles information when you browse the store, place an order, or use the owner's account and email notification service.

Information we collect and why

When you place an order, we collect your name, email address, phone number, delivery address, order notes, selected products and quantities, and payment-method choice. We use these details to process, confirm, deliver and support your order. We record order numbers, order status and payment status to manage purchases and enquiries.

Payment receiving details displayed by the store belong to the owner. The checkout does not ask for your card number, bank password, wallet PIN or one-time payment codes. Do not include these in order notes or messages.

Owner account and Google connection

The owner signs in through Supabase Authentication. Supabase manages the account credentials and session. The owner's notification email is stored separately from public social profile links and payment receiving information.

The owner may authorize Sehar Crafts to send transactional emails through the owner's Gmail account. This uses Google's Gmail send permission and OAuth credentials stored on the server. This integration sends order receipts, confirmations and order/payment-status notifications to the owner and customers. It does not request permission to read the Gmail inbox, contacts, or other messages. Customers do not need to connect a Google account.

Google account data and authorization are used to provide this notification feature, not for advertising or training AI models. Sehar Crafts' use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. The owner can revoke the connection through their Google Account's third-party connections settings; this stops the Gmail notification integration.

Service providers and sharing

GitHub Pages hosts the website. Supabase provides authentication, product storage, order records and notification processing. Google processes emails when Gmail sending is enabled. The notification service also supports Resend if configured by the owner. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies and may process it outside your country. Delivery details may be shared with the delivery provider when needed to fulfil an order.

Browser storage and external links

The site uses browser storage to maintain the owner's login session and a temporary password-recovery verification marker. The shopping bag is held in the current page's memory. The automated store guide gives preset shopping guidance; it is not a live person or a generative AI service. Social media links open third-party websites whose privacy policies apply. The site also loads fonts from Google Fonts, which can receive standard connection information such as your IP address.

Retention and your choices

Order records are retained as needed to fulfil orders, handle support, maintain business records and meet applicable obligations. To request access, correction or deletion of your information, contact the owner at seharcraftss@gmail.com. Include your order number, but do not send passwords or payment credentials. Some records may need to be retained for legal or unresolved transaction requirements.

Keeping your details safe

Owner access is restricted through account authentication and database permissions. Keep your private order tracking/payment link confidential: anyone who has it can view the order information available through that link. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions, email seharcraftss@gmail.com. We may update this page as store features or data practices change.
